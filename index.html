Rules of thumb used on this page, not taken from a single source: default overlaps of 80% front and 70% side (in line with the flight-planning guidance published by Pix4D and Agisoft Metashape); at least 5 photos per spot on the overlap map; an RMS reprojection error under about 1 px for a good alignment; and dense clouds of hundreds to thousands of points per m². The example camera matches the published specifications of the DJI Phantom 4 Pro.

The site, camera and numbers are simulated for teaching. The example camera is a 1-inch, 20 MP sensor (13.2 × 8.8 mm, 5472 px wide) with an 8.8 mm lens, so each photo covers 1.5 × altitude across track and 1.0 × altitude along track. Point densities here are far lower than a real project so the page runs in any browser.
